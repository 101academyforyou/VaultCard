隱私權政策

適用於 VaultCard 網頁版與 iOS / Android App　·　最後更新：2026 年 11 月 24 日

我們不蒐集任何個人資料

VaultCard 不需要註冊帳號，也沒有後端伺服器。我們不會蒐集、傳送或分享你的任何個人資料。App 內沒有廣告、沒有追蹤器，也沒有第三方分析工具。

資料儲存在你的裝置上

你建立的名片、存下的人脈筆記、你寫的備註與標籤，只會儲存在你自己裝置的本機儲存空間（瀏覽器 localStorage / App 內部儲存），不會上傳到任何伺服器。

刪除 App 或清除瀏覽器網站資料時，這些內容也會一併刪除。你可以在「設定與資料」頁面下載備份，或匯出成 Obsidian Vault。

分享名片時

當你分享名片連結或 QR Code，名片內容會直接編碼在連結本身（網址 # 之後的部分，瀏覽器不會把這段送給伺服器）。任何拿到這個連結的人都能看到你在名片上填寫的內容，所以請只填寫你願意公開給對方的資訊。

你存下別人的名片時，那些資料同樣只存在你的裝置上。請尊重對方的隱私，不要把別人的資料用於對方未同意的用途。

相機

在支援的瀏覽器中使用「掃描名片」時，相機畫面只在你的裝置上即時辨識 QR Code，不會錄影、儲存或上傳。

外部連結

名片上的網站、社群等連結由名片主人填寫，點擊後會由你的瀏覽器開啟，該網站的隱私權政策由其經營者負責。

兒童隱私

由於本服務不蒐集任何個人資料，因此也不會蒐集兒童的個人資料。

聯絡我們

如果你對本政策有任何問題，請透過 App Store / Google Play 商店頁面上的開發者聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

VaultCard 為獨立專案，與 Obsidian（Dynalist Inc.）無隸屬關係。

Privacy Policy

Applies to the VaultCard website and iOS / Android apps · Last updated: November 25, 2026

We don't collect any personal data

VaultCard requires no account and has no backend server. We do not collect, transmit or share any of your personal data. The app contains no ads, no trackers and no third-party analytics.

Your data stays on your device

The card you create, the contact notes you save, and the notes and tags you write are stored only in your own device's local storage (browser localStorage / app storage). They are never uploaded to any server.

Deleting the app or clearing your browser's site data also deletes this content. You can download a backup or export an Obsidian vault from the “Settings & Data” page.

When you share your card

When you share your card link or QR code, the card's contents are encoded directly in the link itself (the part after the # in the URL, which browsers never send to a server). Anyone who has the link can see what you put on your card, so only include information you're happy to share.

When you save someone else's card, that data likewise stays only on your device. Please respect their privacy and don't use their information in ways they haven't agreed to.

Camera

When you use “Scan card” in a supported browser, the camera feed is used only on your device to recognize the QR code in real time. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.

External links

Website and social links on a card are provided by the card's owner. They open in your browser, and those sites' privacy policies are the responsibility of their operators.

Children's privacy

Because this service collects no personal data, it does not collect personal data from children.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us through the developer contact details on our App Store / Google Play page.

VaultCard is an independent project and is not affiliated with Obsidian (Dynalist Inc.).
